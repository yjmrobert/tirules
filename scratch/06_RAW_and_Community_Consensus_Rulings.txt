THIS IS NOT OFFICIAL, THIS IS A COLLECTION OF RAW (RULES AS WRITTEN) & COMMUNITY CONSENSUS RULINGS.
Capture
        Q: A player cannot place (or produce) a unit captured by another player. Can a player place their own captured unit without being explicitly instructed to do so?
A: No, but a player can capture their own units and can decide to return them to reinforcements at any time.
Elimination
        Q: Does the Nekro Virus keep the faction flagship abilities of eliminated players?
A: Yes, this follows other rulings on components that stay in play after a player is eliminated, those player’s faction sheets remain in play for this ability.


        Q: Does the Avernus remain in play if the Muaat player is eliminated?
A: Avernus, its token, planet card and legendary ability remain in play.


        Q: Do the void tether tokens remain in play if the Empyrean player is eliminated?
        A: The Empyrean Breakthrough remains in play and the Void Tethers remain functional.


        Q: What happens to a plot if you have an eliminated player’s token on it? Does it return to your hand if it’s the only token that was on it?
        A: If there are no other player tokens on the Plot, it returns to hand.


        Q: Does Galvanize still work when the Last Bastion player is eliminated from the game?
A: Yes, the Galvanized Tokens remain attached to non-Bastion units and they continue the roll one additional die for unit abilities and combat rolls (the Bastion faction sheet remains in play until there are no more Galvanize tokens on the game board).


        Q: What happens to a technology gained via the Deepwrought Scholarate PN, Share Knowledge, if the DWS player is eliminated? Do you still have to return it to your deck at the end of the status phase?
A: The DWS PN remains in play with the technology until status at which point the PN is returned to the box.


        Q: Is the Sever token removed at the end of the status phase if the Crimson Rebellion player is eliminated (however unlikely that is)? What about the breach tokens?
        A: The Sever token and promissory note remain in place until the note removes them in the status phase. Breach tokens also remain in play and active breaches remain adjacent. Inactive breaches may be removed as they only serve as clutter at this point. The Crimson faction sheet remains in play.


        Q: Does the Crimson home system still destroy units that are placed or moved into it after the Crimson player is eliminated.
A: Yes and the Crimson faction sheet remains in play.


        Q: What happens to the action cards stored on Ral Nel breakthrough, Data Skimmer, if the Ral Nel player is eliminated?
        A: The breakthrough is returned to the box and all action cards on it are immediately discarded.
Coexistence
        Q: Can a player score public objectives if they coexist in their own home system?
        A: Yes, coexist’s control counts for any rule regarding “scoring objectives” of which the owning home planets rule is included.

        Q: If a player is Coexisting on Mecatol Rex, can they score Imperial Point with Imperial Primary?
        A: No, Coexistence counts as control for scoring objectives only, this does not include Imperial Point.


        Q: Say a player is fighting a ground combat against a planet's owner, and will then fight another player who is coexisting. Who controls the planet after the first ground combat?
A: Control does not change until the Establish Control step, so it remains with its original owner.


        Q: Are coexisting non-structure units with PRODUCTION blockaded?
        A: Yes, coexisting units are always blockaded.


        Q: If the controller of a planet removes all of their units from it, and two or more other players are coexisting, which player gains control of that planet?
        A: Control resolves to the player next in initiative order (starting with the Active Player) in the Action Phase and in Speaker order in all other phases.


        Q: BOMBARDMENT rolls are made against each player coexisting individually. Does this allow Plasma Scoring etc. to trigger multiple times?
        A: Plasma Scoring is applied to one unit during the bombard step.  Plasma will not trigger multiple times.


        Q: Can a player score Stake Your Claim by coexisting on a planet in a single planet system?
        A: Yes, you control a planet in a system that contains a planet controlled by another player if you are in coexistence.


        Q: Can a player explore a planet they are coexisting on? If so, can hazardous exploration cards be resolved with another player's mech and/or infantry? And can the planet be readied by the coexisting player?
        A: You may not explore a planet owned by another player.


        Q: Coexistence in general, what are the timings of it? Say, you commit your ground forces, and DWS chooses to coexist, do you gain control of the planet immediately, or is it during the establish control step? Do you skip combat?
        A: When DWS uses research teams to coexist, this occurs during the commit ground forces step, so when you get to the ground combat step you skip it, moving straight to the Establish Control step, where you gain control. If there were another player coexisting on that planet before invasion, though, you may choose to end that player’s coexistence (and not DWS) during the commit ground forces step, this will result in a combat on that planet, then after combats are resolved the winner will gain control in the Establish Control step.


        Q: If you don’t skip all the combats and the DWS are already coexisting on the planet they chose to coexist at, can you immediately attack them? What if a third party was coexisting with DWS on their planet when DWS ceded control? Can you attack this third party?
        A: You are not able to attack a player who entered coexistence this turn. Once you commit ground forces, if you own the planet, or win ground combat against the planet’s owner you can attack other players who were already in coexistence at the start of this turn.


        Q: What if a planet owned by DWS gets invaded by the Firmament mechs? DWS chooses to go into coexistence, can the Firmament also choose to go into coexistence? What happens then?
        A: Both coexistences happen in the same timing window, so it will go by active player order, the active player is given the opportunity to coexist first, and if they decline, then the other player may choose to coexist.


        `Q: When does the coexisting player gain control of the planet if the owner leaves or is killed? I.e., the owner gets bombarded, does the coexisting player gain control immediately or during the establish control step? Or if the owner picks up all their units from a planet and then lands them back to invade, do they lose control of the planet to the coexisting player when they pick their units up?
        A: During the Invasion step of a tactical action, control is not exchanged until the Establish Control step. At all other times, control is exchanged immediately after the coexisting player becomes the only player with units on the planet.`


        Q: Can you shoot PDS if you chose to coexist? Do you gain the control over the planet back if you kill all invading forces with these PDS shots?
        A: You may fire PDS defense against the active player’s committed ground forces after entering coexistence. If you have the only units on a planet you exit coexistence and you do not lose control of the planet. (Note you do not lose control of the planet when you enter coexistence until the Establish Control step of the tactical action or after ground combat of a non-tactical action).


        Q: If you have coexisting structures and decide to end coexistence by attacking, but don’t win the ground combat, what happens to the structures? Do you still coexist?
        A: When you end coexistence, you end coexistence for all of your units on that planet, including structures.


                Q: What happens to the units coexisting on a planet if Hacan trades it away with their mech ability?
                A: Coexisting units remain in a state of coexistence as ownership changes through Hacan mech or other game effects, unless they become the only units on the planet. The Hacan mech specifically moves units *after* exchanging control and so allows the gaining player to use Integrated Economy before coexistence would end due to no units on the planet.


        Q: Do the L1Z1X replace coexisting structures when they gain control of a planet?
        A: Yes.


        Q: Do coexisting PDS provide planetary shield for the planet’s owner? Can they fire if a player’s ground forces invade the planet to engage in a combat with the planet’s owner?
        A: Planetary Shield from any units on the planet applies to the planet itself and thus protects all units on the planet. All PDS on a planet may use Space Cannon during Space Cannon Defense against units committed from space by the active player.


Q: Can you scanlink with coexisting units? Do you ready the planet for its owner with Pre-FabArcologies if you’re NRA and coexisting? Can you remove units coexisting with you with a hazardous exploration?
A: You cannot explore a planet owned by another player. You may remove any infantry on a planet you control to use a hazardous exploration, including coexisting units.


        Q: What if a planet with coexisting units is elected via Compensated Disarmament? Does the owner of that planet gain trade goods for the destroyed coexisting units?
        A: The planet controller gains all trade goods.


        Q: Do you exhaust your own planets when you bombard the coexisting units with x89?
        A: Yes.

Neutral Units
        Q: Does a neutral unit use SUSTAIN DAMAGE as early as possible, or only before that unit itself would be destroyed. For example, if there's a neutral destroyer and dreadnought in a system, and they take one hit, does this destroy the destroyer or damage the dreadnought?
        A: Sustain window occurs before assign hits and all units with SUSTAIN will use this to cancel hits before then assigning hits to neutral units in the order presented on the neutral unit reference, prioritizing units lower on the reference card first (for example, hits will always be assigned to neutral destroyers before they are assigned to neutral cruisers).


        Q: How are hits assigned to neutral units outside of combat (for example, SPACE CANNON)?
        A: Speaker decides any decisions for the neutral units that are not hits during combat.

        Q: What happens if there are no more neutral units of a given type available in the supply, and another is required to be placed?
        A: They are not placed.

Action Cards
        Q: What is the interaction between Coup D'etat and Extreme Duress?
        A: Extreme Duress is played and resolved.  The active player, should they opt to resolve a Strategic Action can then have Coup D’etat played. Coup D’etat cancels the action, so the active player has still not performed their “next action” for Extreme Duress. Extreme Duress will remain in play until that player’s next turn, where they must still resolve a Strategic Action or suffer the consequences.


        Q: Can Mercenary Contract be played on your own planet?
A: No, you cannot target harmful effects on your own planets, unless explicitly allowed.


        Q: What happens to a planet’s attachments (exploration / Terraform) if the planet card returns to the deck due to the Mercenary Contract action card being played on it?
        A: The attachments are purged (according to Dane).


        Q: Can you play Crisis on yourself?
        A: Yes, you are a valid target for Crisis.


Q: What’s the Sabotage window for Strategize and Overrule? Is it before or after choosing the strategy card?
A: The player playing Strategize or Overrule will target a legal strategy card before Sabotage is played.


        Q: Exchange Program: Does each player propose where the infantry will be placed before either players agree, or do players first agree to have an infantry placed somewhere, sight unseen?
        A: The agreement includes the planets where infantry will be placed.

        Q: Lie In Wait: Say two players make a deal for one of them to play an action card. Previously, this would have been a binding deal. How does Lie In Wait interact with this?
        A: Lie in Wait would resolve after that action card is played and resolved.

        Q: Mercenary Contract: If the planet becomes neutral, does it get re-explored (or does it re-relic for The Fracture planets) when a player invades later?
        A: Yes, the planet card will be retrieved from the planet card deck and it will trigger both exploration and relic depending on its traits.


        Q: Mercenary Contract: What happens regarding the exhausted/readied status of a legendary planet ability card when a legendary planet is returned and then recontrolled?
A: It is returned from the deck readied.


        Q: Puppets on a String: Can you perform transactions or "Start/end of your turn" abilities?
        A: No, it is not your turn.


        Q: Puppets on a String: If Yssaril plays this, and has Transparasteel Plating, can other players play action cards?
        A: Yes, it is not Yssaril’s turn, they are performing an action outside of their turn.


        Q: Puppets on a String: Can you use Fleet Logistics, Master Plan and Keleres Commander?
        A: Fleet Logistics = no, it is not a turn, only an action.  Keleres = no, it is not a component action, but you can use Keleres commander if the action you perform is itself a component action, Master Plan = yes.


        Q: Puppets on a String: Since it is not your turn, how does that interact with Minister of Peace/Starlancer/other “end that players’ turn” abilities?
        A: Those abilities do not prevent the action being taken, and cannot fully resolve as there is no turn to end.


        Q: Rescue: If the ship is in a gravity rift, does it roll for it?
        A: Yes.
