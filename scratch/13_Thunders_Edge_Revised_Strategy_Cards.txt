[[File:Ti07 strategy 6 warfare.png|thumb|Warfare (Base Game)]]'''Type:''' [[Strategy Cards|Strategy Card]]

'''Initiative Order: '''6

''A player might choose this card to allow'' ''some of their ships to move twice, or to build a unit and move it during the same game round''

The card was updated and re-released in the [[Thunder's Edge]] Expansion.

== Primary Ability ==
[[File:WarfareThunder'sEdge.PNG|thumb|Warfare '''Ω'''  (Thunder's Edge)]]
'''Original {{Edition|Base Game}}''':
*Remove 1 of your [[Command Tokens|command tokens]] from the game board; then, gain 1 command token.
*Redistribute any number of the command tokens on your [[Command Sheet|command sheet]]

'''Updated {{Edition|Thunder's Edge}}:'''
*Perform a [[Tactical Action|tactical action]] in any system without placing a command token, even if the system already has your command token in it; that system still counts as being activated. You may redistribute your command tokens before and after this action.

''Note: The updated card now allows for greater control over your fleet tokens during the tactical action, and allows you to enter a system with your command token in it.''
== Secondary Ability ==
'''Original {{Edition|Base Game}}:'''
* Spend 1 token from your strategy pool to use the [[Production]] ability of 1 of your [[Space Dock|space docks]] in your [[Planets and Systems|home system]].
'''Updated {{Edition|Thunder's Edge}}:'''
* Spend 1 token from your Strategy Pool to use the PRODUCTION abilities of units in your home system.
''Notes:''

* ''For both the original and updated version of the Warfare Secondary Ability, the spent token is not placed in the player's home system, but back into their reinforcements.''
* ''The updated card now allows for all units to use their PRODUCTION ability, not just space docks.''

'''Type:''' [[Strategy Cards|Strategy Card]]

'''Initiative Order:''' 4
[[File:Ti07 strategy 4 construction.png|thumb|Construction (Base Game)]]
''A player might choose this card to produce [[Units#structures|structures]], such as [[Space Dock|space docks]] and [[PDS]] units.''

The card was updated and re-released in both the [[Prophecy of Kings]] Expansion, and the [[Thunder's Edge]] Expansion.[[File:Newconstruction.png|thumb|Construction '''Ω'''  (Prophecy of Kings)]]
== Primary Ability ==
'''Original {{Edition|Base Game}}:'''
*[[File:ConstructionThunder'sEdge.PNG|thumb|Construction  '''Ω Ω''' (Thunder's Edge)]]Place 1 [[PDS]] or 1 [[Space Dock]] on a [[Planets and Systems|planet]] you control.
*Place 1 PDS on a planet you control.

'''Updated {{Edition|Thunder's Edge}}:'''
* Either place 1 [[Structures|structure]] on a planet you control, or use the [[Production|PRODUCTION]] ability of 1 of your space docks.
* Place 1 structure on a planet you control.

== Secondary Ability ==
'''Original {{Edition|Base Game}}:'''
*Place 1 token from your strategy pool in any [[Planets and Systems|system]]; you may place either 1 space dock or 1 PDS on a planet you control in that system.
'''Updated <big>{{Edition|Prophecy of Kings}}</big>:'''

* Spend 1 token from your strategy pool and place it in any system; you may place either 1 space dock or 1 PDS on a planet you control in that system.

'''Updated <big>{{Edition|Thunder's Edge}}</big>:'''

* Spend 1 token from your strategy pool to place 1 structure on a planet you control.

''Notes:''

* ''The updated card from [[Prophecy of Kings]] has the same effect but wording has been changed to make interactions with game components clearer (e.g. abilities that trigger upon spending a strategy token). The updated card from [[Thunder's Edge]] is functionally different than the other two.''         
== Additional Rules  ==

* When using the Secondary ability of the Construction Strategy Card, if a player already has a [[Command Tokens|Command Token]] in the chosen system, the spent token is returned to their reinforcements instead. Then, they place either one PDS or one Space Dock on a planet they control in that system
* If a player does not have enough units in their reinforcements (e.g. All 3 Space Docks from Reinforcements on the Game board), they may remove units from any systems that do not contain one of their command tokens and place them in their reinforcements. Then, they must place this unit on the game board as instructed above
