# Wiki FAQ sweep: entries the site doesn't cover (2026-11-21)

Source: `scratch/wiki_faq.md` (218 entries from 57 fandom wiki pages). The wiki is not a numbered
source. Almost all of its FAQ is copied from source 1 (LRR FAQ pp. 40-44) and source 4 (Unofficially
Official FAQ), and the site already cites nearly all of those. Gaps below are for the owner to consider.

## Gaps with a numbered source
- **F_mahact, Jae Mir Kan** (wiki Mahact #1). *Closed 2026-11-21, no change: obsolete under the TE Construction secondary, which places no token.* Source 4:269: "Can you use the Mahact Agent, Jae Mir Kan, to place a token using the secondary of the Construction Strategy Card without placing a structure / A: The system that you place a token in must have an eligible planet for you to put a structure on."
- **F_arborec, Mitosis** (wiki Arborec #1). Source 2:725: "Does Arborec's Mitosis place an infantry on every planet they control? ... A: No — it places a single infantry on one planet you control each status phase." Probably too simple for a note (the card text already says it).

## Gaps with no numbered source (wiki only)
- F_naalu, Neuroglaive (wiki Naalu #7): the Diplomacy primary does not activate the system, so Neuroglaive does not trigger. (The site has the same pattern for Stymie, F_arborec, and E-Res Siphons, F_jol_nar:40, both cited `*`.)
- F_ul, Hel-Titan (wiki Ul #2): may be bombarded if Planetary Shield is ignored. The site already has F_ul:48, cited `*`; the wiki adds no source.
- F_ul, Geoform (wiki Ul #9): counts as an attachment for Reclaim Ancient Monuments.
- F_creuss, Particle Synthesis (wiki Creuss #7, #8): Sarween Tools applies; produced units may be placed in space or on any planet the player controls in that system.
- F_naaz_rokha, Z-Grav Eidolon (wiki Naaz-Rokha #4): does not count against fleet supply.
- F_firmament (wiki #1, #2): a player cannot place their own control token on a plot card; one plot card can hold several players' tokens.
- F_rebellion (wiki #1, #3): Ahk Ravin may swap with a fighter; other players may use epsilon wormholes.
- F_deepwrought, Doctor Carrina (wiki #4): cannot start coexistence on a planet with no units.
- F_argent, Helix Protocol (wiki Argent #2): units moved must follow anomaly rules. Source 4:153 states this generally (naming Benediction and G'hom Sek'kus, not Helix Protocol).
- C_units, Infantry II (wiki Infantry #2): resurrect roll applies however the infantry is destroyed (e.g. Plague).
- C_units / R_abilities (wiki PDS #4): Bombardment, Anti-Fighter Barrage and Space Cannon are optional.
- C_agendas, Shared Research "Against" (wiki Agenda #11): token placed in home system even if the player controls no planets there.
- C_action_cards, Ghost Ship (wiki Action #18): may place a destroyer in an asteroid field without Antimass Deflectors (placing, not moving).
- F_mentak (wiki Mentak #4): Ipswitch with Van Hauge destruction regains all eligible ships. Unclear answer ("Yes" to a two-part question).

## Obsolete or not a ruling
- Arborec #2 (pre-Thunder's Edge Warfare), Ral Nel #1 (joke), Promissory #5 and Muaat #3 and Action #2 (answered by card text), Ral Nel #2 (conflicts with the Linkship card text "each structure can only be triggered once").
