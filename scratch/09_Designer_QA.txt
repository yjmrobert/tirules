Coexist


    Q: Coexistence Control Default (With Multiple Coexisting Players)
    If multiple players are coexisting on a planet and the player controlling the planet no longer has units on the planet, which coexisting player does control default to?
    A: Control defaults to the next player in order as per ability resolution order by phase (initiative order during action phase, otherwise clockwise)


    Q: Coexistence, Perspective During Scoring
    When scoring objectives, who considers coexisting players as controlling the planets they coexist on?
    A: Only the coexisting player counts themselves as controlling that planet. The player the coexisting player is coexisting with does not count the coexisting player as also controlling that planet, but the coexisting player sees both themselves and the controlling player as controlling that planet when scoring objectives.


    Q: Scoring Public Objectives Coexisting At Home
    Does coexisting on your own home system planet count as controlling that planet for the purpose of being able to score public objectives?
    A: Yes, you may score public objectives even if you coexist on but do not outright control each planet in your home system.


    Q: Coexist and Exploration
    Can you explore a planet via scanlink if you coexist there but do not control the planet? Can you spend another player’s coexisting infantry for red explores like Volatile Fuel Source?
    A: You must control a planet outright to explore it. You cannot spend other player’s units to pay for explore cards’ costs.


        Q: Coexist Control Default (During Invasions)
        If a player is coexisting on a planet and the last of the controlling player’s units are destroyed during invasion by bombardment, does control immediately default to the coexisting player?
        A: During invasion, control default due to coexistence does not occur until the establish control step. The invading player may land ground forces and take control of the planet and the coexisting player will emain in coexistence.


        Q: Coexistence and PRODUCTION
        Can coexisting units use their PRODUCTION ability?
        A: Coexisting units can produce ground forces but are always blockaded and cannot produce ships.


    Q: Coexistence and Structures
    Can structures coexist without ground forces? Can ground forces coexist with structures?
    A: Structures without ground forces may maintain control of a planet with coexisting ground forces. Structures without ground forces may coexist by themselves.


    Q: Deepwrought Scholorate’s Research Team Offensive Use
    Can Deepwrought Scholorate’s Research Team ability be used offensively as well as defensively?
    A: Deepwrought may use their Research Teams ability whether they are committing ground forces on a planet another player controls or if another player is committing ground forces on a planet they control. If Deepwrought uses their ability defensively, the invading player may not engage in ground combat with the Deepwrought on the same activation that Deepwrought enters 
    coexistence.
