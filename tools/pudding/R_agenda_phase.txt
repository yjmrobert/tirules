@4
E2 RulesPal, "Can a speaker who can't vote, such as the Nekro Virus or a speaker who played a rider, still break ties?": "Yes — breaking a tie isn't voting, so the speaker always does it."
I1 LRR 8.19: "If there is a tie … the speaker decides which of the tied outcomes to resolve." No exception for a speaker who cannot vote.
@5
E2 RulesPal, "Does an agenda's outcome affect everyone, or only the players who voted for it?": "Everyone, unless the card itself limits the effect to players who voted a certain way, as Mutiny does. Abstaining doesn't exempt you."
@6
I2 RulesPal, "In what order are agenda-phase secret objectives scored?": "In speaker order, clockwise; it only matters if several players would win from the points, a tie that also goes clockwise from the speaker."
I4 Designer ruling (Xxcha hero resolving agendas during the action phase): "Initiative order determines the winner. Initiative order is always the lowest initiative player onwards, regardless of who the active player is."
@7
E2 RulesPal, "When an agenda outcome and a rider both award points, which resolves first?": "The agenda's outcome resolves first, then the riders that predicted it."
I1 LRR 8.22b: "After resolving the outcome of the agenda, any abilities that were dependent upon predicting the outcome are resolved." LRR 1.20 gives speaker order for simultaneous abilities in the agenda phase.
@8
I1 LRR 1.20: players "take turns resolving abilities starting with the speaker and proceeding clockwise. This process continues until each player has resolved each ability that they wish to resolve during that window." Turn-taking implies one at a time. No source states that the player chooses the order.
